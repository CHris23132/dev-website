FAQ

What is ScamDefend?

ScamDefend is a private verification app that helps trusted people confirm sensitive requests using shared verification phrases.

Does ScamDefend automatically detect scams?

No. ScamDefend does not monitor calls or messages or automatically determine whether something is a scam. It provides a verification method families can use when something seems suspicious.

Does ScamDefend guarantee that I won’t be scammed?

No. ScamDefend provides an additional verification step but cannot guarantee scam prevention.

When should I use ScamDefend?

Before responding to unexpected requests involving money, verification codes, account changes, sensitive information, or urgent situations supposedly involving someone you know.

How do shared phrases work?

Once two users establish a trusted relationship, they receive a shared verification phrase that can be used as an additional identity check.

Can I change my phrase?

Yes. Users can rotate their shared verification phrase.

Is my phrase encrypted?

Phrase data is encrypted before it is stored. This is not the same as end-to-end encryption.

Does ScamDefend contact my bank or the police?

No. ScamDefend is an independent verification tool and does not replace banks, law enforcement, emergency services, or professional advice.

Who is ScamDefend for?

Anyone who wants an additional private verification method with family members, partners, close friends, caregivers, or other trusted people.
