FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need the physical Imagine Book?

Yes. The physical book contains the printed geometric image anchors the app uses for page recognition and AR placement.

Does generation require an internet connection?

Yes. An internet connection is required during AI generation when you intentionally create a stylized artwork from your drawing.

Where are my creations saved?

Completed creations and page associations are stored locally in the app’s device storage on that iPhone or iPad.

Does the app require an account?

No. Imagine Book does not require an account to scan pages, capture drawings, generate creations, or revisit My Book.

What permissions does the app use?

Camera access is used to scan page anchors and photograph drawings. Photo Library access is used only when you choose to import existing artwork. You control these permissions in iOS Settings.

What happens if I delete the app?

Removing the app may also remove locally stored creations and page associations. Imagine Book does not currently provide account-based cloud sync or remote recovery.

Why is my page not being recognized?

Keep the entire printed geometric anchor in view, hold the page flat, use bright even lighting, and avoid glare, folds, or objects covering the marker. Confirm the printed page is clear and has not been resized or distorted.

Which devices are supported?

Imagine Book requires a compatible iPhone or iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17 or later with camera and AR support. A physical device is needed for image tracking.
