Introduction

ScamDefend is a private verification app for families and trusted relationships. This policy explains the kinds of information the app is designed to use so people can invite loved ones and share verification phrases. It is written for App Store transparency and should be confirmed against the live implementation before launch.

ScamDefend is not a public social network. Trusted relationships require an invitation and acceptance. Phrase data is encrypted before it is stored. This policy does not claim end-to-end encryption.

How Information Is Used

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To send and accept trusted-circle invitations
  • To generate, store (encrypted), display, and rotate shared verification phrases for accepted relationships
  • To provide support when you contact us

ScamDefend does not monitor your calls or messages and does not automatically decide whether a request is a scam.
